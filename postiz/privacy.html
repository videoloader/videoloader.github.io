Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy describes how information may be processed when you use Postiz Media (the “Service”), an independently hosted social media management platform.

1. Information we process

Depending on the functionality you use, the Service may process account identifiers and login details, social profile identifiers, OAuth access and refresh tokens, media files, captions, scheduling settings, and necessary security or operational data.

2. TikTok integration

When you choose to connect TikTok, you authorize access through TikTok's own consent interface. Subject to permissions granted and features approved, the Service may request basic TikTok account information and permission to upload video files you select. This informational GitHub Pages website does not receive your TikTok password or authorization tokens.

3. Purposes and recipients

We use information to authenticate users, maintain connected accounts, operate publishing workflows, transfer requested content to chosen platforms, and protect the Service. Selected media and related data are transferred to relevant social platforms when you initiate authorized actions. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process data as required to operate the Service. We do not sell personal information.

4. Storage and security

Data may be stored on the infrastructure used by the Service. Access controls and security measures are used to protect the system; no method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free. Data may be retained as needed for service operation and valid requests, subject to applicable law. We do not promise a fixed deletion period.

5. Choices and deletion requests

Where supported, you may disconnect social accounts through the dashboard or revoke access through the social platform's security settings. To request access, correction, or deletion of personal data, email us at the contact address below. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law.

6. Cookies and technical data

Postiz Media may use essential session cookies and operational logs for authentication, stability, and security. This informational GitHub Pages site does not itself collect TikTok OAuth tokens.

7. Third-party services and changes

Third-party services have their own terms and privacy policies. This policy may change as Service functions evolve; the update date will reflect any revisions.
