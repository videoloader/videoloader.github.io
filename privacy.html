Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VideoLoader ("we", "the service") accesses, uses, stores and deletes information when you use it.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

VideoLoader uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to YouTube channels on your behalf. By using VideoLoader you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information we access and collect

  • Google account authorization tokens (OAuth access and refresh tokens) that allow the service to upload videos to channels you authorize. We request only the scope needed to upload videos (youtube.upload).
  • Basic channel information — channel ID and channel name — so you can choose which channel a video goes to.
  • Content you provide — video files and their metadata (title, description, tags, category, audience setting, scheduled publish time).
  • Your contact email, used only to communicate with you about the service.

We do not access your other videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, or any other data in your Google account.

3. How we use information

Information is used solely to upload the videos you provide to the channels you select, at the time you choose, and to show you the status of those uploads. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Video files and metadata are transmitted only to YouTube through the YouTube Data API as part of the upload you requested.

5. Storage and security

  • Authorization tokens are stored encrypted on our server and are never shown to other users.
  • Video files are stored only until they are uploaded and are automatically deleted within 7 days after a successful upload.
  • Access to the server is restricted to the service administrator.

6. Cookies

VideoLoader does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Only technically necessary session data is used to keep you signed in.

7. Revoking access and deleting your data

You can revoke VideoLoader's access to your Google account at any time via the Google security settings page. Once access is revoked, the service can no longer upload to your channels.

To have all stored data deleted (tokens, pending videos, metadata, contact email), email us at the address below. We delete it within 30 days. Stored tokens are also deleted when you revoke access or stop using the service.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and notify users by email.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: pm.videoloader@proton.me
