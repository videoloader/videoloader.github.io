Scheduled publishing of short videos to your own YouTube channels

VideoLoader is a small, free, invite-only tool for independent creators who run several themed YouTube channels. It uploads finished short videos to the creator's own channels on a schedule the creator sets, using the official YouTube Data API.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in with Google and grants permission to upload videos to the channels they own or manage.
  2. The creator adds finished videos together with a title, description, tags, category, audience setting ("made for kids" or not) and a publish time.
  3. At the scheduled time VideoLoader uploads the video to the selected channel through the YouTube Data API.
  4. The creator can revoke access at any time in their Google Account settings.

What VideoLoader does not do

  • It does not read, download or analyze other people's videos or channels.
  • It does not post comments, likes, subscriptions or any engagement actions.
  • It does not modify or delete existing videos on a channel.
  • It does not sell or share user data and shows no ads.

Who can use it

Access is by invitation for a small community of creators. The tool is provided free of charge.
