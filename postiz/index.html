How TikTok sharing is intended to work

  1. Sign in to Postiz Media and choose the TikTok integration.
  2. Authorize your own account using TikTok’s official consent flow.
  3. Select a video you are entitled to share and initiate a transfer when the integration is available.
  4. Review and complete the publication within TikTok.
